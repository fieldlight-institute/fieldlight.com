About

Fieldlight Institute is founded and led by writer and systems builder Anni McHenry.

The Institute develops public research, working infrastructure, and institutional methods for human-owned intelligence systems.

Anni’s work moves between essays, institutional research, and the design of human-owned AI systems. Her professional experience includes complex operational change across behavioral health systems, EHR implementation, software delivery, product workflows, and cross-functional execution.
