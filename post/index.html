A title is optional. The photo button in the form lets you choose images from your phone or computer. Add a short description so people using a screen reader can enjoy them too.

Write in the Post box, tick Publish this post publicly on Fieldlight, then select Create. Leave the prefilled “[Post] New post” label as it is; your public title is optional. It saves your words and photographs in public-writing, then updates Fieldlight and the Everything feed. A status link appears underneath your post. Fieldlight checks for updates about every ten minutes; GitHub’s queue can take longer.
