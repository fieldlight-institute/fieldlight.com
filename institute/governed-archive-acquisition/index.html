What would it mean to compensate people for longitudinal AI archives without turning private life into inventory?

Two boundaries organize the entire method.

01 / Identity boundary Anonymous by default. A participant becomes known only by explicit choice.
02 / Access boundary No human reads the transcripts. Not for valuation, troubleshooting, selection, or research.

Identity-Blind Research Architecture

The person and the corpus travel on separate rails.

Payment administration may need a legal identity. Research does not. The join between those systems is governed, minimal, and unavailable to analysis.

No Fieldlight human reads participant transcripts.That rule has no exception inside this protocol.

The Acquired Unit

A license.
A custody relationship.
Not the person.

The archive may enter research. The participant does not become inventory.

Payment recognizes structural depth, continuity, completeness, and the participant’s labor. It does not purchase a life, reward exposure, or convert possession into unlimited authority.

Archive Components

The export is not one undifferentiated object.

Conversation, agentic work, and the files those systems touched carry different risks and different evidentiary value. They stay separately declared, hashed, valued, and permissioned.

The Acquisition Passage

Authority is named at every threshold.

  1. 01Invitation

    Purpose, possible use, pay, security, and choices arrive first.

  2. 02Participant export

    The participant keeps account control and chooses source components.

  3. 03Preflight

    Structure and integrity are inventoried without transcript reading.

  4. 04Offer + grant

    The score, compensation, permissions, and prohibitions become visible.

  5. 05Secure accession

    The source stays unchanged; any working copy receives separate governance.

  6. 06Bounded computation

    Only named systems, operations, outputs, purposes, and periods apply.

  7. 07Participant record

    Custody, actions, derivatives, corrections, and active grants remain inspectable.

  8. 08Closeout

    Every source, copy, index, finding, and unresolved derivative is accounted for.

Evidence Architecture

Three layers.
No silent passage.

01 / RestrictedPreserved source

Original package, manifests, hashes, source-specific permission, and completeness record.

02 / GovernedResearch derivatives

Inventories, normalization, indexes, embeddings, analysis, and annotations under named authority.

03 / AuthorizedPublic derivatives

Participant-selected excerpts, aggregate findings, methods, diagrams, and publications.

Research authority does not become publication authority. Public authority does not flow backward into the source.
