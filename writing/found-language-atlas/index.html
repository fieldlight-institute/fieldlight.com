A photographed phrase is only the visible knot. Each encounter joins a symbolic system already moving through notebooks, fiction, field writing, photographs, and later formal work. The page follows relations, not isolated signs.

What the atlas already shows

Attention has a grammar.

01

Language acts

Most entries advise, command, refuse, warn, name, or vow. They attempt to alter the reader, not merely decorate a surface.

02

Surface authorizes

A corporate slip, private notebook, mural, window, and altered sidewalk give words different kinds of permission and risk.

03

Photography transports

The original address moves from passerby-time into archive-time. The most secure fact created by selection is attention.

04

An encounter can exceed one source

Ghirardelli is visible in the drone image; the first-SF-coyote memory lives in creator context. Together they define the event without pretending either record was complete.

05

Difference makes recurrence useful

“Growth” matters because its two appearances conflict productively. SALTY matters because a market label intersects an already-established public name.

06

Unknown remains a field

Analytic fluency does not replace creator memory. A blank motive is more accurate—and more useful—than an invented one.

07

Selection authority is evidence

The analyst held Strawberry Love; the creator restored it. The correction exposed a category failure and improved the atlas's unit from text alone to word, image, and creator naming.

08

Recurrence can create a new object

WINNOW and MISSIONWINNOW make a pair whose archival meaning depends on two independent surfaces. Their return creates a third object: the relation itself.

09

Creator context can unlock a motif

The analyst saw a strawberry pictogram. The creator knew its autobiographical life. The files did not contain the decisive relation; review supplied it.

How the atlas grows

Every entry remains subordinate to its source. Context can be added later; uncertainty is preserved rather than paved over.

Locate or preserve the source before interpreting it.
Assign an atlas ID without renumbering the source.
Transcribe spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and line breaks as seen.
Separate encounter, capture, and archive dates.
Record general place; suppress private coordinates.
Name surface and apparent voice, with uncertainty.
Invite creator context; never manufacture it.
Connect echoes as relations, not automatic derivations.
Keep evidence, interpretation, and causal claims separate.
Leave unknowns blank, stable, and visible.
